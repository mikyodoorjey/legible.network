Hippius (SN75)

Decentralised cloud storage subnet launched in March 2025 by The Nerve Lab, running its own Substrate chain and Arion erasure-coded storage with an S3-compatible front end; its 2025 writing calls it the storage Bittensor lacked, its 2026 docs sell storage without AWS and barely name Bittensor at all.
