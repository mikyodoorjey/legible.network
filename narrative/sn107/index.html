Minos (SN107)

Genomic variant-calling subnet launched in March 2026 that turns finding DNA mutations into a 72-minute winner-take-all competition scored against synthetic genomes with hidden mutations; it calls itself the foundational layer of genomics, says accuracy is the only thing that matters, and is ranked first by emission in September 2026.
