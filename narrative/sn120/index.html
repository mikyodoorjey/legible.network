Subnet. Affine Foundation · SN120 · Subnet 120

Affine (SN120)

Reinforcement-learning subnet founded in mid 2025 and run as a king-of-the-hill competition where miners upload model checkpoints and the reigning champion takes the emission; led by Bittensor co-founder Jacob Steeves after he left the Opentensor Foundation, so its language is the closest any subnet gets to the founder's own.

SubnetSubnet team; Jacob Steeves (Const), CEO of Affine and Bittensor co-founder, speaks for itAffine Foundationactive since 2025@affine_iogithub AffineFoundationSN120 in the index
