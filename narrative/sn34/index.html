BitMind (SN34)

Deepfake and AI-content detection subnet launched in 2024, rebuilt in late 2024 as GAS, a generative adversarial engine where generator miners forge and detector miners catch; its enterprise site sells a trust layer and rarely says Bittensor, while its founder tells the press Bittensor is the decentralised network where hundreds of developers compete to build the best detector.

SubnetSubnet team; Ken Jon Miyachi, co-founder and CEO, speaks for it; Dylan Uys, co-founder and head of AIBitMind Holdings Limitedactive since 2024@bitmindgithub BitMind-AISN34 in the index

BitMind's mission is to create the best deepfake detection solution on the market, demonstrating the potential of decentralized AI and building the infrastructure for AI engineers to develop powerful end-user applications.
